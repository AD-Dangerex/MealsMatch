01 — WHY FOODBRIDGE

There is enough food.
We need a better way
to connect it.

Restaurants, schools and catering services often have surplus food at the end of the day. At the same time, charities and community organisations are looking for reliable food supplies.

FoodBridge creates a direct connection between them, helping surplus food reach people who can use it.
